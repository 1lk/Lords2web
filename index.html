Menu

  • Tap — click
  • Hold a finger — right-click (shows info about what you touch; right-click again, or just tap, to close it)
  • Two-finger tap — right-click
  • Pinch / two-finger drag — zoom & pan the picture
  • Scroll pad (bottom-left) — scroll the campaign map; on a keyboard use the arrow keys or touch the screen edge

Demo — native WebAssembly port

The game's code runs here as WebAssembly, recompiled from the original program — no emulator. Its graphics, sound and maps are not part of this page: give it your own copy of the demo installer.

The installer is unpacked in your browser and kept in its local storage. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
